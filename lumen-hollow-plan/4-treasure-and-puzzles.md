# Milestone 4 — Treasure + Brain-Based Puzzles

Read `@plan/00-OVERVIEW.md` first.

## Goal

Add a treasure objective that cannot be reached by simply running and jumping.

The player should need observation, exploration, timing and logical thinking.

## Puzzle mechanics

Use appropriate combinations of:
- Switches
- Switch sequences
- Pressure plates
- Movable crates
- Hidden passages
- Secret rooms
- Keys
- Locked doors
- Moving platforms
- Temporary bridges
- Enemy movement as part of a solution

Puzzles must have understandable clues.
Do not rely on random guessing.

## Exploration

Reward exploration with:
- Hidden rooms
- Secret passages
- Secret coins
- Optional collectibles
- Environmental clues
- Alternate routes

## Treasure

The treasure should:
- Be visually noticeable
- Have a satisfying reveal/open animation
- Produce sound and particle effects
- Be inaccessible until the required puzzle path is completed

The final treasure should become increasingly difficult to reach across the three levels.

Do not make an impossible puzzle.
Do not copy Nintendo level designs.

## Test

Verify:
- Treasure is not reachable by brute force
- Puzzle solution is logically discoverable
- Switches/keys/doors reset correctly on level restart
- Exploration areas work
- No soft locks

Do not commit.
